## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Agents describe how to work with them
Every default agent preset SHALL have capabilities stating what the agent does, what it needs as input, what it
produces and who reviews it; creating an agent SHALL recommend them, and a team lead SHALL receive its team's
roster with these capabilities in its brief.

#### Scenario: New specialist added to a team
- **WHEN** an owner adds a 3D artist preset to a team
- **THEN** the agent SHALL come with capabilities saying it needs a 2D concept first and is reviewed by the game
  designer, and the lead's next brief SHALL list it with them
