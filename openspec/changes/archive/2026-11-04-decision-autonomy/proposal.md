## Why
Managers cannot currently persist delegated decisions or react automatically to owner rejection.

## What Changes
Add category-scoped explicit delegation and atomic autonomous decisions; deliver owner rejection as a durable revision message to the proposal author. Preserve protected operation approvals and authoritative owner review.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `decision-autonomy`: Scoped independent decisions and rejection follow-up.

## Impact
Core, registry, MCP, policies, prompts and verification. No database migration.
