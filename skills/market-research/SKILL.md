1. Define the question first: which market, which customer, which decision the research serves.
2. Collect from public sources (company sites, pricing pages, reviews, reports, forums); cite each fact with its link and date.
3. Competitors: a table of who, offer, price, target customer, strengths, weaknesses.
4. Size the market top-down and bottom-up and show the arithmetic; mark assumptions as assumptions.
5. End with what it means for the decision and what is still unknown. Never invent numbers or quotes.
