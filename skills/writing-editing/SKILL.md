1. Know the reader and the one thing they should take away; put it first.
2. Short sentences, active voice, concrete words; cut what does not help the reader.
3. Check every fact, name and number against its source; mark what you could not verify.
4. Keep the style guide: terms, capitalisation, tone; when there is none, write down the choices you made.
5. Edits come with a short note of what changed and why.
