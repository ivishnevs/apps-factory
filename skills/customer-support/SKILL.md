  eval: "Answer a customer who cannot log in after changing their email. Good: a polite answer with concrete steps, no credential requests, a tag, escalation when needed."
---

1. Read the whole request and the customer's history before answering; answer the question they asked.
2. Plain words, short paragraphs, the steps to take, and what happens next. No blame, no jargon.
3. Never promise refunds, dates or features you cannot grant; route those to a person with the facts collected.
4. Tag every request (bug, how-to, billing, feature) so patterns show; a problem seen three times becomes a task or a help article.
5. Never ask for or repeat passwords or card numbers.
