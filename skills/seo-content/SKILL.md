1. Find what people search for (keywords, questions) and the intent behind it; one main topic per page.
2. Structure: a clear title, headings that answer the questions, a short summary first, then details.
3. On-page: title tag, meta description, descriptive links, image alt text, fast pages.
4. Write for people first: accurate, specific, with examples; no keyword stuffing, no copied text.
5. Track rankings and visits per page and update what underperforms.
