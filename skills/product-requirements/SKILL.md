---

1. Start from who the user is and what problem they have; write it in one paragraph before any solution.
2. Scope: what is in, what is explicitly out, what comes later.
3. Requirements as testable statements with acceptance criteria (given / when / then); every requirement has a reason.
4. Name the metric that tells whether it worked and its target.
5. List open questions with your recommended answer for each, so a person can just confirm.
6. Keep it short: a page that people read beats ten they skip.
