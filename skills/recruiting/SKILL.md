  eval: "Prepare hiring for a junior support specialist. Good: role outcomes, a job post, a scored rubric, a note that people decide."
---

1. Start with the role: outcomes in the first 6 months, must-have and nice-to-have skills, level, salary range if known.
2. Job post: plain, specific, inclusive; what the person will do and how they will be evaluated.
3. Screening and interviews: the same questions for everyone, a rubric with what good looks like, scores before discussion.
4. Keep candidate data private and only for the hiring decision; no protected characteristics in notes.
5. Hiring decisions are people's: prepare the evidence, do not decide.
