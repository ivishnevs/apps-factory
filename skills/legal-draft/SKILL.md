1. Every output is a draft marked 'not legal advice, for review by a qualified lawyer'.
2. Use the jurisdiction and parties given; ask when they are missing instead of guessing.
3. Plain structure: definitions, obligations, payment, liability, termination, governing law.
4. Flag risks and unusual clauses in a list next to the draft, with why they matter.
5. Never sign, send or accept anything on anyone's behalf.
