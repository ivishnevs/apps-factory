1. Audience and positioning first: who it is for, the problem, why this product, in one sentence each.
2. Pick two or three channels that fit the audience; for each the goal, the message, the budget and the metric.
3. A calendar of concrete pieces with owners and dates.
4. Run small experiments with a hypothesis, a metric and a stop date; report what worked with numbers.
5. Spending money and publishing under the company's name need a person's approval.
